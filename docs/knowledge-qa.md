# Ask your retained knowledge

Open the chat panel and keep **Ask knowledge** selected. Ask about a decision, current work, preferences or a reusable lesson. Select a project to restrict the answer to its retained context, or keep **All projects** for a cross-project question. English is the default; all controls also support Simplified Chinese through Settings.

The answer includes record cards and original-source buttons. **View record** opens the existing knowledge detail dialog; a source filename opens its original content in that same viewer. Imported evidence, your corrections, historical information, conflicts and context-only sources keep their own labels. Model prose is **model interpretation**, and optional deductions are **model inferences**. A cited record does not independently verify every sentence generated by the model.

Specific questions with no matching retained evidence receive an insufficient-evidence reply without a model request. Retrieval is deterministic and lexical: use a project name or a term from your retained knowledge if an indirect question finds nothing. There is no second conversation-extraction pass, embedding service or whole-vault prompt. Current records are preferred unless the question explicitly asks about history or previous decisions.

## Bounds, privacy and recovery

- One latest question, at most 2,000 characters; at most 8 records and 12,000 record-text characters. Up to 3 representative sources per record are displayed, with the full source list available through the record viewer.
- One answer request, plus at most one repair for invalid structured output or unknown record IDs. Existing Anthropic/OpenAI-compatible model, HTTP proxy and system-network settings apply. Provider-reported tokens are recorded; prices are not calculated.
- Durable corrections, preference validity and source privacy exclusions apply before retrieval. Excluded content stays on disk but is not admitted to the model context. Local source paths are not included in the question prompt.
- A knowledge, correction or privacy change during an answer suppresses the old result. Citations carry a revision of the record, original source metadata and source policy, and are checked again before navigation.
- **Stop waiting** cancels the browser wait and prevents late results or further local repair. A request already sent to the provider may still complete within its timeout and use tokens. You can ask again; knowledge records are never edited by asking a question.

Choose **Conversation** for general discussion or advice. Existing score-card discussion actions retain their conversation behavior. Knowledge answers and questions are kept out of the later conversation prompt. Drafts and structured citation cards survive a local page remount; previous answers may require a new question when their source revisions change.

## Implementation

The pure retrieval and output rules live in knowledge/qa_retrieval.py and knowledge/qa_output.py. app/knowledge_qa.py composes corrected/privacy-filtered library records, revision guards and a two-request usage allowance. providers/knowledge_qa.py uses the existing configured transport with a forced answer tool and a 30-second timeout. The private chat owner renders results with text-safe DOM operations and delegates citation navigation to the existing knowledge browser.

The authenticated loopback routes are GET /__knowledge_qa_context__, POST /__knowledge_qa__, and GET /__knowledge_qa_citation__. The POST rejects ambiguous or oversized body framing before reading it. Synthetic unit, loopback provider and native-browser tests cover unsupported questions, provenance, privacy changes, stale citations, cancellation and both languages. These checks do not claim semantic entailment or real-world factual verification of every generated answer.
